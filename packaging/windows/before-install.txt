OpenKnowledge answers questions from your documents, on this machine.

What this installer does:

  - Installs OpenKnowledge for your user account only. No administrator
    rights, no services, nothing outside your own profile.
  - Includes llama.cpp's inference server (MIT license), which runs the
    models: on your GPU where a Vulkan driver exists, on your CPU otherwise.

What happens on first launch:

  - The app downloads the two models it answers with, about 2.6 GB once:
      Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Apache-2.0), the chat model
      nomic-embed-text-v1.5 (Apache-2.0), the retrieval model
    Both are verified against pinned SHA-256 hashes - the exact files the
    project's published accuracy numbers were measured on.
  - A private data folder is created under your user profile. Your
    documents never leave this machine; nothing phones home.

The chatbot then opens in your browser and lives in the system tray.
