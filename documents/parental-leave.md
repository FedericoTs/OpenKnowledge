# Parental Leave

Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service are entitled to 20 weeks of fully
paid parental leave. Employees with less than 12 months of service are entitled to 6 weeks
at statutory rate.

Requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance through the HR portal. Unused
parental leave does not carry over into the following year.

Leave may be taken in a maximum of three separate blocks.
