# Expenses Policy

## Approval thresholds
Any single expense above EUR 500 requires prior written approval from your line manager.
Expenses at or below EUR 500 may be submitted directly without pre-approval.

## Meals and subsistence
Meals are reimbursed up to EUR 45 per day when travelling on company business.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances, including client entertainment.

## Submission deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days require a written exception from Finance.
