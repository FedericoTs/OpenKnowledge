# Northwind Logistics — Expense Approval Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

## Approval limits

A line manager may approve expenses up to **EUR 500** per claim. Any claim
above EUR 500 must be countersigned by Finance before payment.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every claim above EUR 25.
