# Northwind Logistics — Annual Leave

Owner: People Operations.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive **25 days** of annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays.

## Carry-over

A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the following year. They expire
on 31 March.
