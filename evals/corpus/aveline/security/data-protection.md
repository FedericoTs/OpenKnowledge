# Data Protection Policy

**Owner:** Data Protection Officer · **Version:** 4.0 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Roles

Aveline Group is the data controller for employee and customer personal data.
Where Aveline processes personal data on behalf of a client, Aveline is a
processor and the client's instructions govern.

## 2. Personal data breaches

### 2.1 Notification to the supervisory authority

A personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and
freedoms of individuals must be notified to the Data Protection Commission
within **72 hours** of Aveline becoming aware of it.

Where notification is made later than 72 hours, it must be accompanied by
reasons for the delay.

### 2.2 Notification to individuals

Where a breach is likely to result in a **high** risk to individuals, those
individuals must be informed without undue delay. Notification to individuals is
not required where the data was encrypted and the key was not compromised.

## 3. Data subject requests

A data subject access request must be answered within **one month** of receipt.
The period may be extended by **two further months** where the request is complex
or where several requests have been received from the same individual; the
individual must be told of the extension within the first month.

Requests are free of charge. A fee may be charged only where a request is
manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 4. Retention

Personal data is retained for no longer than is necessary:

| Record type | Retention period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Payroll records | 7 years | Revenue requirement |
| Unsuccessful applicant CVs | 12 months | Legitimate interest, defence of claims |
| CCTV footage | 30 days | Security |
| Customer transaction records | 6 years | Statute of limitations |
| Marketing consent records | 3 years after withdrawal | Accountability |

Data is deleted or anonymised at the end of the period. Where a legal hold is in
place, deletion is suspended until the hold is lifted.

## 5. Transfers

Personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area only under
standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. Transfers must be recorded
in the transfer register maintained by the Data Protection Officer.
