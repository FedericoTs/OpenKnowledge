# Travel Guidelines

**Owner:** Facilities & Travel · **Version:** 2.3 · **Effective:** 12 January 2026

## Booking travel

All travel must be booked through the Aveline travel desk, which holds the
negotiated corporate rates. Travel booked outside the desk may be refused
reimbursement.

## Approval

Travel expenditure above **EUR 1,000** requires written approval from a line
manager before booking. Below that amount, staff may book directly through the
travel desk without prior approval.

## Accommodation

Hotel spend is capped at EUR 180 per night in Dublin, London and Paris, and
EUR 140 per night elsewhere. Where no room is available within the cap, the
travel desk may authorise an exception.

## Ground transport

Taxis may be used where public transport is unavailable, where the journey is
outside 07:00-22:00, or where luggage makes public transport impractical.
Ride-hailing applications are acceptable where a receipt is issued.

## Visas and vaccinations

Visa fees, travel vaccinations and travel insurance are reimbursable in full and
do not count against the meal or accommodation caps.
