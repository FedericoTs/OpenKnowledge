# Expenses Policy

**Owner:** Finance Operations · **Version:** 4.1 · **Effective:** 1 March 2026
**Supersedes:** Expenses Policy v3.0 (January 2023)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Aveline Group and to contractors engaged
directly by Aveline. It does not apply to agency workers, whose expenses are
handled by their agency under a separate agreement.

## 2. Approval

### 2.1 Prior approval threshold

Any single item of travel expenditure above **EUR 500** requires written approval
from a line manager **before** the expense is incurred. Expenditure at or below
EUR 500 may be claimed without prior approval.

### 2.2 Delegated approval limits

Approvers may authorise expenses up to the limit for their grade:

| Grade | Approval limit | Escalates to |
|---|---|---|
| Analyst | EUR 500 | Manager |
| Manager | EUR 1,500 | Director |
| Director | EUR 5,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Chief Financial Officer | EUR 25,000 | Board |

An approver must not authorise their own expenses, regardless of grade.

## 3. Subsistence

### 3.1 Meals

The meal allowance is **EUR 45 per day** for domestic travel within Ireland and
**EUR 65 per day** for international travel. The allowance covers all meals for
that day and is not itemised. Where a meal is provided as part of a conference
fee or by a client, the allowance is reduced by EUR 15 for that meal.

### 3.2 Alcohol

Alcohol is **not reimbursable**. The single exception is client entertainment
that has been approved in advance in writing by a Director; in that case
reasonable alcohol costs may be included in the entertainment claim.

## 4. Travel

### 4.1 Mileage

Where a private vehicle is used, mileage is reimbursed at **EUR 0.42 per
kilometre** for the first 5,000 km in a calendar year and EUR 0.28 per kilometre
thereafter.

### 4.2 Rail and air

Standard class rail must be booked wherever the journey is under four hours.
Economy class air travel is mandatory for flights under six hours. Business class
may be booked for flights of six hours or more with Director approval.

## 5. Submission

Claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days will not be paid except where the delay was caused
by prolonged sick leave or parental leave.

Receipts are required for every item above EUR 25. Below that threshold a
description is sufficient.

## 6. Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid in the payroll run following approval. Payroll runs on
the 25th of each month, or the preceding working day where the 25th falls on a
weekend or public holiday.
