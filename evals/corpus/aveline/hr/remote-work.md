# Remote Working Policy

**Owner:** People Operations · **Version:** 2.1 · **Effective:** 1 February 2026

## Eligibility

All employees who have completed probation may work remotely for up to
**three days per week**. Employees in client-facing roles may work remotely for
up to **two days per week**, because client coverage is expected on site.

Employees still within probation must work on site full time.

## Location

Employees must work from within their country of employment. Working from another
country requires prior written approval from People Operations and may have tax
consequences that Aveline will not indemnify.

Requests to work from another country for more than 20 working days in a rolling
12 months will not be approved.

## Equipment

A one-off home equipment allowance of **EUR 500** is available on joining, and
again after four years. It covers a desk, a chair and a monitor. It does not
cover broadband, heating or electricity.

Laptops and phones are issued by IT and are not purchased through the allowance.

## Core hours

Employees working remotely must be contactable during core hours of
10:00 to 16:00 in their country of employment.
