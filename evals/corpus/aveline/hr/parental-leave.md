# Parental Leave Policy

**Owner:** People Operations · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## Entitlement

Employees with **12 months of continuous service** at the expected date of birth
or placement are entitled to **20 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.

Employees with less than 12 months of continuous service are entitled to
**12 weeks at full pay**, followed by statutory pay for any remaining statutory
entitlement.

Contractors are **not eligible** for company-paid parental leave. Contractors
should refer to their engagement terms.

## Notice

Employees must give at least **30 days notice** in writing before the intended
start of leave. Where the birth or placement occurs earlier than expected, notice
must be given as soon as is reasonably practicable.

## Taking leave

Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first 24 months
after birth or placement. Each block must be at least two consecutive weeks.

## Return to work

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to the same role.
Where the role no longer exists, People Operations must offer a suitable
alternative on no less favourable terms.

A phased return of up to four weeks at 80% hours on full pay is available on
request and does not require Director approval.
