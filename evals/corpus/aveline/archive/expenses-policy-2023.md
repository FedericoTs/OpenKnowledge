# Expenses Policy (2023)

**Owner:** Finance Operations · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 1 January 2023
**Status:** SUPERSEDED by Expenses Policy v4.1 (March 2026). Retained for audit only.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Aveline Group and to contractors engaged
directly by Aveline.

## 2. Approval

### 2.1 Prior approval threshold

Any single item of travel expenditure above **EUR 300** requires written approval
from a line manager before the expense is incurred.

### 2.2 Delegated approval limits

| Grade | Approval limit | Escalates to |
|---|---|---|
| Analyst | EUR 300 | Manager |
| Manager | EUR 1,000 | Director |
| Director | EUR 3,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Chief Financial Officer | EUR 15,000 | Board |

## 3. Subsistence

### 3.1 Meals

The meal allowance is **EUR 35 per day** for domestic travel within Ireland and
**EUR 50 per day** for international travel.

### 3.2 Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## 4. Travel

### 4.1 Mileage

Mileage is reimbursed at **EUR 0.38 per kilometre** for the first 5,000 km in a
calendar year and EUR 0.25 per kilometre thereafter.

## 5. Submission

Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the date the expense was incurred.
Receipts are required for every item above EUR 20.

## 6. Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid in the payroll run following approval. Payroll runs on
the 28th of each month.
