# Procurement Policy

**Owner:** Chief Financial Officer · **Version:** 3.4 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Authority to commit

No employee may commit Aveline to expenditure without approval at the correct
level. Approval must be obtained **before** any commitment is made, including a
verbal commitment or an accepted order form.

| Contract value (annual) | Approver | Additional requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Up to EUR 5,000 | Line manager | None |
| EUR 5,001 to EUR 25,000 | Head of Department | Written business case |
| EUR 25,001 to EUR 50,000 | Chief Financial Officer | Three competitive quotes |
| Above EUR 50,000 | Board | Three quotes and legal review |

Splitting a purchase to stay below a threshold is a disciplinary matter.

## 2. Competitive quotes

Three competitive quotes are required for any contract with an annual value above
**EUR 25,000**. Where fewer than three suitable suppliers exist, the Chief
Financial Officer may waive the requirement in writing, recording the reason.

## 3. Third-party ICT services

A supplier of an ICT service that supports a critical or important function is
subject to enhanced due diligence before contracting, covering:

- financial standing and ownership;
- subcontracting chains and their locations;
- security certifications and the date of the most recent audit;
- exit arrangements and data return format;
- concentration risk across the group.

Contracts for such services must include audit rights, incident notification
obligations and a defined exit period of no less than **six months**.

## 4. Payment terms

Standard payment terms are **30 days** from receipt of a valid invoice. Terms
shorter than 30 days require Chief Financial Officer approval. Terms longer than
60 days must not be agreed.

## 5. Signing

Only a director of Aveline Group may sign a contract. A Head of Department may
sign a purchase order within their approved limit but may not sign a contract,
a master services agreement or a data processing agreement.
